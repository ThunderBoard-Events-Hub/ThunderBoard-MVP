Community guidelines

ThunderBoard Community Guidelines

To keep the ThunderBoard community safe, inclusive, and reliable for all students, all clubs and organizations must review and accept these community guidelines before publishing their flyers and events to the app.

Posts that violate these guidelines may be flagged, hidden, or permanently removed from the public feed. Repeated or severe violations will result in the temporary or permanent suspension of your organization’s posting privileges.

Thank you for being an active and respectful participant in this community. We appreciate you!

Safety and inclusivity

  • Zero tolerance for hate speech. Content that promotes discrimination, harassment, hate speech, or violence based on race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or religion is strictly prohibited.
  • Respectful imagery. Visuals must not contain explicit, sexually suggestive, or graphic violent imagery.
  • Safety protocols. Events involving specific age restrictions (e.g., 19+ socials) or high physical activity must clearly state these requirements in the event description.

Accurate and authentic event details

  • Real events only. All posted flyers must correspond to actual, scheduled events hosted or co-hosted by your organization.
  • Essential information. Every flyer upload must include accurate date, time, location (building/room), and host organization details.
  • Timely updates. If an event is cancelled, postponed, or relocated, update or archive your listing immediately.
  • No spam or duplicate posts. Do not upload duplicate flyers for the same event. Please edit your listing if major event details change.

Copyright and intellectual property

  • Original & licensed assets. Ensure your club owns or has authorization to use all graphics, logos, photos, and media embedded in your uploaded poster.
  • Misrepresentation. Do not claim affiliation with unauthorized university departments, third-party brands, or other clubs without express permission.
  • Sponsorship compliance. Third-party corporate logos or event sponsors must be legally authorized by your organization and comply with campus partnership standards. Promoted products must adhere to legal age restrictions.
